    S-1 Grid help

    A pattern grid and sound editor for the Roland AIRA Compact S-1. It runs in your browser, sends MIDI to the S-1 and doesn't change its firmware. Your patterns stay on this computer. It needs a current Chrome or Edge on a computer. It's unofficial and not affiliated with Roland.

    Visits to this page are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies. It never receives your patterns or patches.

    Set up

    1. Connect the S-1 to your computer with a USB-C data cable, and turn it on.
    2. Allow MIDI access when asked. The MIDI badge turns green when the S-1 is found. If it says MIDI blocked, click the icon to the left of the address, set MIDI devices to Allow, then click Allow MIDI.
    3. Click a note name in the grid. You should hear it on the S-1.
    4. Check that Channel at the bottom matches the S-1's MIDI channel. It's 3 unless you've changed it.

    Working in a key

    • Choose a Key and Scale. The grid only shows notes in that key, so anything you draw fits. The key's root note is highlighted.
    • Move notes with key moves all of your patterns into a new key when you change it, keeping each note's place in the scale. Turn it off to leave notes where they are.
    • All notes shows every note. Notes outside the key are marked in their row name.
    • Draw places a single note, a power chord, a triad or a 7th, using notes from the key. To hear chords, set Polyphony to Poly on the VOICE tab.
    • Idea and Generate write a bassline, melody, arpeggio or chord pattern in the key. Generating replaces the current pattern, and Undo brings it back.
    • Oct - and Oct + move every note in the current pattern down or up an octave.

    Sound controls

    • The panel on the right has every control the S-1 accepts over MIDI, grouped into tabs. Changes are immediate, even during playback.
    • The sound stays the same when you switch patterns. Only loading a patch or a saved sound changes it, and saving a pattern saves the sound as it is then.
    • Turning a knob on the S-1 updates the matching control here. A value shown as ? hasn't been set or received yet, because the S-1 can't report its settings when asked.
    • Save to library, below the controls, saves the current pattern and sound together as a patch in your library folder. Values shown as ? keep the value from the patch you loaded, or otherwise the blank patch's.
    • The drawn wave's shape, and settings with no MIDI control, can only be changed on the S-1.

    Patch library

    • The LIBRARY tab keeps patches as files in a folder you choose on this computer. Each holds a full S-1 pattern: notes, motions, and every sound setting.
    • Import backup fills it from an S-1 BACKUP folder, with its patterns that have notes and one blank patch. These are whatever is saved on your S-1, which may include your own edits.
    • Click a patch to load it: the grid fills with its notes, its sound controls update, and its sound is sent to the S-1. The play button also starts playback. A patch's key is estimated from its notes.
    • Save to library saves the current pattern and sound as a patch, and works with no notes if you just want to keep a sound. Loading a patch fills in its name, so saving updates it after asking. Type a new name to keep both.
    • Names are entirely up to you: use Name to rename a patch. The S-1 doesn't store names, and its files have fixed names by slot, which the page handles when saving.
    • To send several at once: tick patches in the list, then click Send to S-1. Each patch gets a row with its own slot. They start on the next empty slots. To change one, select its row and click a slot, or use its list. The same patch can go to several slots with Add to list.
    • To S-1 on a single patch opens Save to S-1 with that patch selected.
    • When saving, hovering over a slot shows which library patch it holds, if its contents exactly match one.
    • Some settings have no MIDI control, like delay feedback or a drawn wave, so they won't be heard during live playback. They're kept in the file and work once a patch is on the S-1. Motions play live here too, as a close match. Step sub-divisions don't show or play here, but moving a patch to the S-1 keeps them.

    Patterns and the S-1's own memory

    • Patterns 1-16 here are stored in this browser, and clearing its data for the site erases them. Export MIDI keeps a copy as a file.
    • Import MIDI replaces the current pattern with the notes from a MIDI file, and Undo brings it back. All its tracks are merged, leaving out drums on channel 10 if there's anything else. It also sets the tempo and estimates the key. The pattern gets 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars, whichever fits, and anything past 8 bars is cut. A patch loaded into that pattern no longer applies, though the sound stays.
    • Patterns play from this page, so the page must stay open.
    • Save to S-1 writes a pattern and sound into the S-1's memory, using its USB backup and restore. It takes a restart of the S-1, and the button walks you through it. This isn't a method Roland documents, so keep a copy of the S-1's BACKUP folder.
    • A save holds up to 64 steps, 4 notes per step and values on 1/16 steps. Anything else is rounded or left out, and you're told first. Long notes are saved as tied steps. Settings shown as ? stay as they are in the pattern's source. Pan and mod wheel aren't stored in patterns. A pattern loaded from the library keeps its motions, while other saves remove any motions in the slot being replaced. Library patches sent directly are copied exactly.
    • Record to S-1 plays a pattern once for the S-1 to record instead: select a pattern on the S-1, press REC, then PLAY, then click the button soon after. Timing depends on how it records, and this hasn't been confirmed on the hardware.
    • Send clock starts the S-1's own sequencer as well, so you may hear both unless its pattern is empty.

    If you hear nothing

    • Check that the MIDI badge is green, and reload the page if the S-1 was off when it opened.
    • Check the channel, and that the S-1's volume and filter Cutoff are up. Filter cutoff at minimum gives no sound.
    • Use Panic if a note gets stuck.

    Save to S-1

    This saves patterns and sounds onto the S-1, with no recording.

    1. Turn the S-1 off. Then hold PLAY while turning it on.
    2. Wait 1-2 minutes for the S-1 drive to appear on your computer. The pads show the progress.
    3. Choose the drive here.

    Choose a slot for each patch

    Select a patch, then click a slot on the S-1 below, or choose one from its list.

      Slots on the S-1

      Bank 1-4 and pattern 1-16. Outlined slots already have notes. Clicking a slot gives it to the selected patch.

        1. Eject the S-1 drive on your computer, and wait for it to disappear.
        2. Press HOLD on the S-1.
        3. Wait for dOnE, then turn the S-1 off and on again.

        Restore writes all 64 patterns. The other 63 are copied from the S-1's current saved versions, so they shouldn't change.
