    S-1 Grid help

    A pattern grid and sound editor for the Roland AIRA Compact S-1. It runs in your browser, sends MIDI to the S-1 and doesn't change its firmware. Your patterns stay on this computer. It needs a current Chrome or Edge on a computer. It's unofficial and not affiliated with Roland.

    Visits to this page are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies. It never receives your patterns or patches.

    Set up

    1. Connect the S-1 to your computer with a USB-C data cable, and turn it on.
    2. Allow MIDI access when asked. The MIDI badge turns green when the S-1 is found. If it says MIDI blocked, click the icon to the left of the address, set MIDI devices to Allow, then click Allow MIDI.
    3. Click a note name in the grid. You should hear it on the S-1.
    4. Check that Channel at the bottom matches the S-1's MIDI channel. It's 3 unless you've changed it.

    Working in a key

    • Choose a Key and Scale. The grid only shows notes in that key, so anything you draw fits. The key's root note is highlighted.
    • Move notes with key moves all of your patterns into a new key when you change it, keeping each note's place in the scale. Turn it off to leave notes where they are.
    • All notes shows every note. Notes outside the key are marked in their row name.
    • Draw places a single note, a power chord, a triad or a 7th, using notes from the key. To hear chords, set Polyphony to Poly on the VOICE tab.
    • Idea and Generate write a bassline, melody, arpeggio or chord pattern in the key. Generating replaces the current pattern, and Undo brings it back.
    • Choosing an Idea also sets every sound control to a good starting point for it, such as a round, punchy tone for a bassline. Init does the same for the idea already chosen. Hear original, under the sound controls, brings back the sound you had. Generating doesn't change the sound, so you can try more patterns without losing your tweaks.
    • Sine sets every control to get as close to a sine wave as the S-1 can. It has no sine oscillator, so this is a square wave through the low-pass filter, with full key follow. Lower Cutoff for a purer but quieter tone, or raise it if notes get thin. Very high and very low notes may need different settings.
    • Shift moves the whole pattern a step earlier or later, wrapping around. Scale moves every note a step through the scale, and Oct moves them an octave.
    • Click or drag along the step numbers above the grid to choose steps. Copy (or Cmd+C) copies their notes. Choose where they go, in this pattern or another, then Paste (or Cmd+V), which replaces what's there. Click a chosen step again or press Esc to clear.
    • Velocity and Set change every note in the chosen steps, or the whole pattern if none are chosen.

    Sub-steps, chains and recording

    • Sub-step, when you right-click a step, plays it in quick repeats using the S-1's 14 patterns, where o plays and - is silent. They play here, save to the S-1, and show on the note.
    • Chain takes pattern numbers such as 1 1 2 3. Tick On and Play goes through them in order, looping. Clicking a pattern during playback plays it next, then the chain carries on.
    • Rec records from the S-1's keys. While playing, notes land on the nearest step and last as long as you hold them. While stopped, each key adds a note and moves on a step, starting from the chosen step. Undo takes back the whole take.

    Drawing knob movements

    • Knobs opens a lane under the grid. Choose a knob and drag to draw how it moves through the pattern, or Alt-drag to erase. The S-1 stores 8 points per step and up to 8 knobs per pattern.
    • They play from this page and save to the S-1 as motions, as well as to the library. Shift moves them along with the notes.
    • Copy all to copies every knob's movements in this pattern, including a loaded patch's, to another pattern, replacing any it has. The notes aren't copied, and Undo takes it back. Movements past the end of a shorter pattern are left out.
    • A loaded patch's motions show here too. Drawing replaces its recorded motions with what's shown when you save, and keeps any pitch bend movements.

    Sound controls

    • Vary nudges the sound's levels a little or a lot, leaving tuning, pan and switches alone. Hear original flips between the sound before you started and the new one.
    • The panel on the right has every control the S-1 accepts over MIDI, grouped into tabs. Changes are immediate, even during playback.
    • The sound stays the same when you switch patterns. Only loading a patch or a saved sound changes it, and saving a pattern saves the sound as it is then.
    • Turning a knob on the S-1 updates the matching control here. A value shown as ? hasn't been set or received yet, because the S-1 can't report its settings when asked. Init or loading a patch fills them in.
    • Save to library, below the controls, saves the current pattern and sound together as a patch in your library folder. Values shown as ? keep the value from the patch you loaded, or otherwise the blank patch's.
    • The drawn wave's shape, and settings with no MIDI control, can only be changed on the S-1.

    Patch library

    • The LIBRARY tab keeps patches as files in a folder you choose on this computer. Each holds a full S-1 pattern: notes, motions, and every sound setting.
    • Hear plays a patch at its own tempo with its own sound, without changing the grid. Your sound comes back when it stops, apart from any controls showing ?, as the page doesn't know what they were.
    • ☆ marks a favourite, and Favourites only shows just those. Tags adds words that the search also finds. Both are saved in a library-info.json file in your library folder.
    • Import from S-1 lets you pick which of the S-1's 64 patterns to copy into the library, and name each one. It reads the S-1's USB drive, or a BACKUP folder you saved, and doesn't change anything on the S-1.
    • Import backup fills it from an S-1 BACKUP folder, with its patterns that have notes and one blank patch. These are whatever is saved on your S-1, which may include your own edits.
    • Click a patch to load it: the grid fills with its notes, its sound controls update, and its sound is sent to the S-1. The play button also starts playback. A patch's key is estimated from its notes.
    • Save to library saves the current pattern and sound as a patch, and works with no notes if you just want to keep a sound. Loading a patch fills in its name, so saving updates it after asking. Type a new name to keep both.
    • Names are entirely up to you: use Name to rename a patch. The S-1 doesn't store names, and its files have fixed names by slot, which the page handles when saving.
    • To send several at once: tick patches in the list, then click Send to S-1. Each patch gets a row with its own slot. They start on the next empty slots. To change one, select its row and click a slot, or use its list. The same patch can go to several slots with Add to list.
    • To S-1 on a single patch opens Save to S-1 with that patch selected.
    • When saving, hovering over a slot shows which library patch it holds, if its contents exactly match one.
    • Delay sync on the FX tab has no MIDI control, so it takes effect once a pattern is saved to the S-1. Loading a patch fills it in. With sync off, Delay time shows ms. With sync on, it shows a note value, one of 16 from 1/128 to 1/4, as the S-1's knob sends in that mode. Either one is what's saved. If the slider doesn't match the knob, set Delay sync to match the S-1.
    • Some settings have no MIDI control, like delay feedback or a drawn wave, so they won't be heard during live playback. They're kept in the file and work once a patch is on the S-1. Motions and sub-steps play live here too, as a close match.

    Patterns and the S-1's own memory

    • Patterns 1-16 here are stored in this browser, and clearing its data for the site erases them. Back up saves all 16, their patches and the sound to one file, and Restore loads it back. Export MIDI keeps one pattern's notes as a file, with sub-steps written as separate notes.
    • Import MIDI replaces the current pattern with the notes from a MIDI file, and Undo brings it back. All its tracks are merged, leaving out drums on channel 10 if there's anything else. It also sets the tempo and estimates the key. The pattern gets 1, 2, 4 or 8 bars, whichever fits, and anything past 8 bars is cut. A patch loaded into that pattern no longer applies, though the sound stays.
    • Patterns play from this page, so the page must stay open.
    • Save to S-1 writes a pattern and sound into the S-1's memory, using its USB backup and restore. It takes a restart of the S-1, and the button walks you through it. This isn't a method Roland documents, so keep a copy of the S-1's BACKUP folder.
    • A save holds up to 64 steps, 4 notes per step and values on 1/16 steps. Anything else is rounded or left out, and you're told first. Long notes are saved as tied steps. Settings shown as ? stay as they are in the pattern's source. Pan and mod wheel aren't stored in patterns. A pattern loaded from the library keeps its motions, and drawn knob movements are saved. Other saves remove any motions in the slot being replaced. Library patches sent directly are copied exactly.
    • Record to S-1 plays a pattern once for the S-1 to record instead: select a pattern on the S-1, press REC, then PLAY, then click the button soon after. Timing depends on how it records, and this hasn't been confirmed on the hardware.
    • Sync plays this page together with MPC Grid when both are open in the same browser, each with its MIDI connected. Pressing Play or Stop on either one starts or stops both, from the start of their patterns, at the tempo of the page you pressed. Changing the BPM on either changes both. Patterns of different lengths each loop in their own time. Untick it on either page to play them separately. The badge beside it shows whether the other page is connected.
    • Send clock starts the S-1's own sequencer as well, so you may hear both unless its pattern is empty.

    If you hear nothing

    • Check that the MIDI badge is green, and reload the page if the S-1 was off when it opened.
    • Check the channel, and that the S-1's volume and filter Cutoff are up. Filter cutoff at minimum gives no sound.
    • Use Panic if a note gets stuck.

    Save to S-1

    This saves patterns and sounds onto the S-1, with no recording.

    1. Turn the S-1 off. Then hold PLAY while turning it on.
    2. Wait 1-2 minutes for the S-1 drive to appear on your computer. The pads show the progress.
    3. Choose the drive here.

    Choose a slot for each patch

    Select a patch, then click a slot on the S-1 below, or choose one from its list.

      Slots on the S-1

      Bank 1-4 and pattern 1-16. Outlined slots already have notes. Clicking a slot gives it to the selected patch.

        1. Eject the S-1 drive on your computer, and wait for it to disappear.
        2. Press HOLD on the S-1.
        3. Wait for dOnE, then turn the S-1 off and on again.

        Restore writes all 64 patterns. The other 63 are copied from the S-1's current saved versions, so they shouldn't change.

        Import from S-1

        This copies patterns and sounds from the S-1 into your library. Nothing on the S-1 changes.

        1. Turn the S-1 off. Then hold PLAY while turning it on.
        2. Wait 1-2 minutes for the S-1 drive to appear on your computer. The pads show the progress.
        3. Choose the drive here. A BACKUP folder you saved earlier works too.

        Click the patches to import
